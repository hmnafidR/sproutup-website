Privacy Policy 🌱

Sprout Up! · a Sproutine game
Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what information the Sprout Up! game (“the game”, “we”, “us”) handles on Android and on the web, and the choices you have. The short version: we do not collect or sell your personal information. Your progress stays on your device, and worldwide top scores on Android are handled by Google Play Games only if you choose to sign in.

1. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things in your device's storage:

  • your best score, daily play streak and the date you last played
  • your ☀️ sunshine balance and the Wardrobe items you own and wear
  • whether sound is on or off

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, clear the app's data, or clear your browser's site data. On Android, your phone's own backup (for example Google backup) may include it, under your Google account's backup settings.

2. Google Play Games (Android only)

The Android app uses Google Play Games Services for the worldwide Top scores leaderboard. If you are signed in to Play Games, Google receives your scores and links them to your Play Games profile (your gamer name and avatar) so they can appear on the leaderboard. Google processes this information under the Google Privacy Policy. We can see leaderboard entries the same way other players do, but we do not receive your email address or other account details.

Play Games is optional: you can play without signing in, and you can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings.

3. The web version

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads its font from Google Fonts. Like any website, these services receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type to deliver the page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy. The web version does not use cookies for tracking.

4. What we do not do

  • No accounts with us, no sign-up forms, no email collection.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising IDs.
  • No ads at the moment. If we add ads in a future version, we will update this policy first and show any required consent choices in the game.
  • We never sell or share personal information.

6. Children

Sprout Up! is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Your choices

  • Delete your local progress by uninstalling the app or clearing its data (or your browser's site data).
  • Play without signing in to Play Games, or sign out at any time.
  • Manage or delete your Play Games profile through Google.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, for example by adding ads or new online features, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email us at sproutineapp@gmail.com.
