Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data resets your score, streak, sunshine and Wardrobe.

How do I earn ☀️ sunshine? 1 for every 10 points, +2 for each Routine Complete, and a daily bonus on your first run of the day.

How do I get on the Top scores board? On Android, sign in to Google Play Games, then tap 🏆 Top scores on the menu.
